# SplitIt Groups: planning session (2026-11-26)

> Feature request (user, 2026-11-26): "support something like venmo groups - where you can add multiple receipts and transactions lets say for a trip or something to a group and it calculates net money owed to people - can you help me plan and design this feature"
>
> Method: `.claude/skills/app-planner` (four personas, Phases 0–5). **Verified** = checked on 2026-11-26 at the linked page. **Unverified** = from a search snippet, a third-party blog, or a store listing I didn't open. **Assumption** = our reasoning. Nothing here is built. Decisions marked **(you)** are yours: they involve money rules, a `CLAUDE.md` rule, or are hard to reverse.

---

## TL;DR

- A **Group** (a trip, a house) holds **receipt bills** (scanned or typed and claimed by item, the same as today) and **simple expenses** ("Ana paid $240 for the Airbnb, split 4 ways"), plus **payments** recorded between members.
- **Balance = what you paid − your share + payments you sent − payments you got.** It's computed in `lib/` from data the app already loads, with integer cents. Balances always add up to exactly $0.
- **Settle up** suggests at most n−1 payments (greedy, deterministic). Each one gets a Venmo Pay link (for the person who owes) or a Request link (for the person owed), one amount per link. "I paid" records a payment.
- **"Pay the difference" comes for free.** If a bill is edited after people have paid, the balances move and a new small payment is suggested. No `settle_difference` is needed inside groups.
- **The biggest thing that changes:** bills in a group stop settling one by one. The group settles them all together.

## Phase 0: Framing (PM)

**Who:** friends on a trip or a weekend away (3–10 people, 5–40 costs over a few days), and later housemates.
**Problem:** today every SplitIt bill settles on its own. On a trip that means a dozen Venmo payments going both ways ("I owe Ana $30 for dinner, she owes me $45 for gas"). Costs that aren't receipts (Airbnb, gas, tickets) have no place in the app at all.
**Solution:** one group that collects everything, nets it out, and suggests the fewest payments, each one opened in Venmo with the amount filled in.

**Assumptions the idea depends on**
| # | Assumption | Risk if false |
|---|---|---|
| A1 | People want receipts claimed item by item *and* quick lump-sum expenses in one place | If only lump sums matter, we're a worse Splitwise |
| A2 | Everyone on the trip will sign in (ADR 0004) | Trips always include "the friend who won't download an app" (open question Q2) |
| A3 | Self-reported payments are good enough (ADR 0015) | Disputes; but every competitor works the same way |
| A4 | One currency per group is enough for v1 | International trips need multi-currency (v2) |
| A5 | Venmo-only links are acceptable for now | Groups with non-Venmo users fall back to "pay another way" |

No blocking questions, so I proceed on these assumptions. The open questions are at the end.

### Adjacent alternatives
- **A shared spreadsheet or Notes list** that one person tallies at the end. Free, but one person does all the work and errors are common.
- **Pay as you go** in Venmo, one payment per cost. Lots of small payments.
- **One person pays for everything** and requests an even split at the end. Simplest, and unfair when orders differ.
- **Cash App / Zelle / PayPal:** no group ledgers found that matter here. Relevant only as later link targets (ADR 0007).

### How others build it (prior art)
- **What Splitwise's simplify promises** ([Splitwise KB](https://kb.splitwise.com/balances-and-expenses/what-is-simplify-debts), **verified**): it "never changes anyone's total balance — it only optimizes the payment paths". It reruns on every new expense or payment, and each currency is handled separately. It does **not** promise that you only pay people you directly owe. Its own example has Anna paying Charlie.
- **Fewest payments is NP-hard** ([Verhoeff et al., arXiv 1111.3663](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1111.3663)). It reduces to finding the largest number of disjoint zero-sum subsets. Greedy matching of the largest creditor with the largest debtor always settles in **≤ n−1** payments and is a 2-approximation ([nimral/splitbill](https://github.com/nimral/splitbill), informal source). An exact search over subsets is possible for small groups (2ⁿ subsets; fine up to about n ≤ 12).
- **Known pitfalls** (from the sources above, plus our own lessons):
  - **Multi-currency:** Splitwise keeps a balance per currency.
  - **Rounding:** without largest remainder, balances drift by a cent.
  - **Edits after payment:** simplified routes change, so people "resend funds to the right person" (Splitwise KB).
  - **Routing to strangers:** simplification can ask you to pay someone you didn't share a cost with, and that person may not have Venmo.

### Gaps and opportunities
1. **Nobody found combines item-by-item receipt claiming with a running group ledger.** Splitwise has Itemize and groups, but Itemize turns a receipt into a single expense (**assumption**, not verified in detail). Venmo Groups doesn't claim items.
2. **No daily cap, no ads, free.** Splitwise's cap is the wedge. Tricount and Spliit already offer this, so it isn't unique.
3. **A ledger fixes our own rough edges.** Negative shares ("Settle that outside the app"), pay-backs and differences all become ordinary balance changes.

### Platform and regulatory constraints
- **No money moves in-app** (ADR 0007), so no money-transmitter questions. Recording "Sam paid Ana $50" is a note, like Venmo's "Mark as paid".
- **Venmo links carry one amount and one recipient** (ADR 0007 note 2026-11-25; the user asked and confirmed this). So it's one Pay or Request per suggested payment, never "request all".
- **Venmo notes can be public**, so a group name in the note may be visible on a public feed.

**What this means for us:** a group ledger is table stakes, and three competitors give it away free with no cap. Our only real difference is **claiming receipts item by item inside the ledger**, plus Venmo deep links. The MVP should make "scan dinner → everyone claims → it lands in the trip balance" feel effortless, and make simple expenses take under 15 seconds. We should not try to out-feature Splitwise (categories, charts, recurring).

## Phase 3: Product Manager, critical questions

**Is the problem real?** Yes. Trips are the classic case for Splitwise, and Venmo built Groups and called it "one of our most requested features" ([Fox10](https://www.fox10phoenix.com/news/venmo-groups-splitwise-alternative-bill-calculator), headline, **unverified**). For us there's another signal: we already hit the limits of settling one bill at a time (negative shares, pay-backs, differences). That's internal evidence, not user research.

**Why would someone pick us?** Only for *item-by-item claiming inside a trip ledger, with no cap*. If claiming items isn't valued on trips (A1), we lose to Tricount and Venmo Groups. **The riskiest assumption is A1 + A2: will a whole trip group sign in and claim items?**

**The smallest MVP that tests it:** groups, putting bills in a group, simple expenses split equally or by exact amounts, balances, suggested payments with Venmo links, and recording payments. Cut everything else.

**Out of v1:** multi-currency, recurring expenses, categories and charts, exports, placeholder (non-account) members, push notifications or reminders, an exact fewest-payments solver, PayPal/Cash App, receipt photos on simple expenses, comments.

**Monetization:** free. Unit cost is about $0: Supabase only, no per-expense cost, and no Edge Function (ADR 0010). A later paid tier could copy Settle Up's **one-off Group Premium** (exports, recurring, multi-currency) rather than a cap. **Never cap expenses.** That's the complaint we'd be winning on.

**Success metrics (rough targets, assumptions)**
| Metric | Target |
|---|---|
| Activation: groups with ≥ 3 members and ≥ 3 costs within 7 days | ≥ 50% of created groups |
| The mix: groups with ≥ 1 claimed receipt *and* ≥ 1 simple expense | ≥ 40% (tests A1) |
| Settling: groups where ≥ 1 suggested payment is recorded within 14 days of the last cost | ≥ 50% |
| Trust: balance-dispute reports per 100 groups | < 1 |
| Retention: hosts who create a second group within 60 days | ≥ 25% |

**Risks (likelihood × impact)**
| Risk | L | I | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Non-app friends block whole groups (A2) | H | H | Q2: placeholder members in v2, or a "settle with X outside the app" line |
| Simplification confuses people ("why pay Cy?") | H | M | Show "Who paid what" next to the suggestions. v1: a toggle to pay only people you owe directly |
| Balances jump after edits, after people paid | M | H | Activity explains every change. Notice: "Balances changed since your last payment" |
| A balance off by 1¢ between phones (different app versions) | L | H | Deterministic math. Property tests. Version-pin `lib/split.ts` behavior |
| Data load too big (PostgREST `max_rows = 1000`, `supabase/config.toml:19`) | M | H | One jsonb snapshot function per group (Phase 4) |
| Incumbents (Venmo) add item claiming to Groups | M | H | Ship fast. Make claiming the best part |
| Privacy: members see everything | M | M | Defined in "Privacy" below. Venmo note kill switch |

**Kill criteria**
- Under 25% of groups ever contain a claimed receipt after 2 months (A1 is false: claiming items doesn't matter on trips). Then pivot to "SplitIt = the best single-bill splitter" and drop groups.
- Under 30% of invitees sign in and join (A2 is false). Then do placeholder members before anything else.
- Any confirmed case where the balances don't sum to $0, or two phones show different numbers. Stop shipping until it's fixed.

**Top 5 open questions** (repeated in Phase 5 with actions)
1. **(you)** Should bills in a group stop settling one by one, with any existing "I paid" marks turned into group payments when a bill moves in?
2. **(you)** Is it OK that people without SplitIt can't be on a trip (ADR 0004), or do we need placeholder members sooner?
3. **(you)** Should incomplete receipt bills count toward balances? Recommended: proportional bills count, with unclaimed items sitting with the payer; even-split bills wait until complete.
4. **(you)** Should Settle up show simplified payments by default, with "Who paid what" one tap away? Or pay-only-people-you-owe?
5. **(you)** Should joining a group count as a "link" for People you've split with (rule L1, ADR 0016), and can all group members see each other's Venmo handles?

## Phase 4: Engineer, implementation brainstorm

### Where balances are computed: architecture options
| Option | How | Pros | Cons |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A. Balances in `lib/` from raw rows** (recommended) | The app loads the group's bills, claims, expenses and payments, runs `computeShares` per bill, and nets everything in a pure `lib/groupBalance.ts` | Reuses `computeShares` as-is. No split math in SQL. The DB already "can't compute shares" (ADR 0021 §3), so this is the same stance | The DB can't enforce "leave only at $0". Large groups need a smart fetch |
| B. Port `computeShares` to SQL; balances in a view | `private.bill_shares(bill)` in plpgsql | The DB could enforce balance-based rules | A second copy of the hardest money code (proportional, even, claimable discounts, rule Z, CD-6/7/8). The same-rule convention means testing both on every case forever. The user rejected "import, never copy" (lesson 2026-11-25) but still wants the same rule tested both sides. High cost, low benefit, because a payment is honor-system anyway |
| C. Snapshot shares | The app writes each bill's computed shares to `bill_shares`. The DB checks only Σ = total | Cheap reads | Trusts client-written money. Snapshots go stale on every claim or edit. A malicious participant could move shares within the sum |

**Recommendation: A.** The DB stores facts (who paid, who claimed, which payments were recorded) and enforces only rules it can check without split math. The app computes the money. That's the same pattern as settling today.

**No custom backend needed.** Everything is tables, RLS, grants and security-definer SQL functions (ADR 0013). Push reminders would need a server or scheduled job (v1+). **That's a backend decision for you** when we get there.

### Stack (unchanged)
Expo + Expo Router + TypeScript; Supabase Postgres/RLS/Realtime; Venmo links through `lib/paylinks.ts`; styling with `lib/theme.ts` and `components/ui/` (ADR 0020).

### Data model sketch
```
groups            id, name (1–60), created_by → profiles, currency 'USD' (check, like bills),
                  join_code (20 hex, DB-generated, like bills), created_at
group_members     (group_id, user_id) PK, joined_at, added bool   -- added=true: added by a member, not via link (L1)
bills             + group_id uuid null → groups                    -- a bill is in 0 or 1 group
expenses          id, group_id, title (1–100), payer_id, amount_cents 1..99,999,999,
                  split_method 'equal'|'exact'|'shares', created_by, created_at, edited_at, deleted_at null
expense_shares    (expense_id, user_id) PK, weight int null (1..20 for equal/shares), cents int ≥ 0
group_payments    id (client-generated uuid = idempotency key), group_id, from_user, to_user,
                  cents 1..99,999,999, recorded_by, source 'manual'|'venmo'|'bill_mark', created_at, deleted_at null
group_events      like bill_events: append-only activity (expense_added/edited/deleted, bill_attached,
                  payment_recorded/undone, member_joined/left …)
group_hidden      (group_id, user_id) owner-only, like bill_seen   -- "archive for me", never shared
```
Invariants:
- Every participant of a group bill is a group member (enforced in functions).
- The payer and every share user of an expense is a member.
- A payment's `from_user ≠ to_user`, and both are members.
- Money is integer cents (ADR 0005).
- All sums use bigint (the settle_state lesson).

### Key design decisions (Engineer proposes; PM reacts in Phase 5)
| Topic | Recommendation | Alternatives | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Bill ↔ group** | `bills.group_id`, 0 or 1 group. You can add a bill when you create it ("Add to Tahoe") or move one in later from the bill screen (host only). Host can remove it from the group, with a confirm "Balances will change" | Many groups per bill (double-counting); groups only for new bills | 0/1 is unambiguous. Moving bills in later covers "we scanned it before making the group" |
| **Bill participants vs members** | When a bill joins a group, **every member becomes a participant** (`added = true`). A new member is added to every group bill. Someone who wasn't at dinner just doesn't claim and owes $0 for it. Proportional and even modes already give non-claimers $0 (ADR 0014) | A new RLS rule "group members can read group bills" | Keeps the `CLAUDE.md` rule "only participants can read a bill". Claims, Realtime and profile reads all work unchanged. Cost: a "Dee was added" line on each bill. Don't log events for adds that come from the group |
| **Bill participants who aren't members** | Moving a bill in requires its participants to already be members (show "Invite Sam to Tahoe first") | Auto-add them to the group | Nobody joins a group without consent (ADR 0016 spirit) |
| **Per-bill settling inside a group** | Off. The bill's Settle screen says "Settled in Tahoe" and links there. `mark_paid` / `settle_difference` refuse with `22023 in_group`. Moving a bill in **turns existing marks into payments** (`ower → payer`, `settled_cents`, `source = 'bill_mark'`) in the same transaction | Refuse to move bills that have marks | Nothing is lost, and history stays in Activity. Two ways to settle the same bill would double-count |
| **Which bills count toward balances (rule G)** | Use rule E (ADR 0021) as-is. `final` bills count in full. **Proportional incomplete bills count, with unclaimed rows charged to the payer** for now. **Even-split incomplete bills don't count** (their totals are hidden, ADR 0014). `all_zero` bills don't count. The group screen lists "Waiting on claims: Brunch (2 items left)" | Only complete bills count | Matches what settling early already allows. Σ stays $0. Avoids a big swing when a host adds a row to a complete bill |
| **Simple expense split** | MVP: **Equally** (choose who) and **Exact amounts** (must add up exactly, the same "adds up" style as the bill form). v1: **Shares** (1–20 parts, like items) | Percentages | Covers "split 4 ways" and "my own ticket". Shares reuse `allocate` |
| **Who adds or edits** | Any member adds an expense or bill. An expense can be edited or deleted by **its creator or its payer**. Receipt items stay host-only (`CLAUDE.md`). Delete is soft, so Activity keeps it | Anyone edits (Venmo/Splitwise style) | Predictable, and close to "host edits items". Activity shows every change |
| **Balance math** | `net(u) = Σ paid(u) − Σ share(u) + Σ payments sent(u) − Σ payments received(u)`. Shares come from `computeShares` (bills) and `expense_shares.cents`. Property: Σ net = 0, exactly | — | Pure, integer, testable |
| **Simplification** | Greedy: biggest debtor pays biggest creditor. Ties go to the lower user id (code-unit order, like `split.ts`). Skip nets with \|net\| ≤ `SETTLE_DIFF_IGNORE_CENTS` (1¢, SE-11). ≤ n−1 payments. v2: an exact zero-sum-subset search when n ≤ 12 | Fewest payments (NP-hard); pairwise only | Deterministic, so every phone shows the same list |
| **Two views** | "Suggested payments" (simplified) plus **"Who paid what"**: per person *paid / share / net*, and tapping a person shows their costs. Pairwise debts in v1, with a pay-only-people-you-owe toggle | A pairwise view only | Answers "why do I pay Cy?" without a second set of numbers |
| **Payments** | "Pay Ana $130.02 on Venmo" → "Did you pay Ana?" → "I paid" records a payment for **the amount opened** (contract O, ADR 0021 §6). The person owed gets "Request $130.02" (no write). **"Record a payment"** takes any amount: partial, cash, or overpaying with a warning. Either side of a payment can record it ("Got paid" for cash) and either can undo it | Only the sender records it (SE-5) | Cash on trips is common. Undo by either side matches ADR 0015 Q4 |
| **Edits after paying** | No special mechanism. The ledger moves and a new suggestion appears. Notice: "Balances changed since you paid: Ana edited Dinner" | Reuse `settle_difference` | That's the whole point of a ledger |
| **Currency** | USD only (`check (currency = 'USD')`, as on bills). Code takes currency as a parameter (ADR 0005) | Multi-currency per expense | v2 needs exchange rates and per-currency balances (Splitwise's model) |
| **Leaving or removing** | Rule L3-G, in SQL: you may leave or be removed only with **no history** in the group (no claims on group bills, not a payer, no expense shares, no payments). Otherwise **"Hide group"** (owner-only `group_hidden`). Checkable without split math | "Leave at $0 balance" | A $0 check needs balances in SQL (option B). Leaving never changes money (L3) |
| **Invites** | Group link `/g/<code>` with the QR (`components/InviteQr.tsx`), Share…, From contacts (ADR 0016 §6). "Add from People you've split with" (linked only, `not_linked` hides existence) | Email invites | Everything reused |
| **Linking (L1)** | Joining by link, or having any expense/claim, makes you *active*. Active members of a group are linked | Groups don't link | Same consent signal as bills |
| **Privacy** | Members see all of the group's bills, items, claims, expenses, payments and each other's Venmo handles (`profiles` select policy widened to co-members). Venmo note for group payments: `SplitIt: <group name>` behind a kill switch (like `VENMO_NOTE_INCLUDES_ITEMS`). Hide and dismiss state is owner-only | Hiding per-bill details from non-attendees | Same as on a bill today. Added to `docs/before-production.md` §6 |

### Core flow (end to end)
1. Create a group → `create_group(name)` → you're the only member. Share the link or QR.
2. Friends open `/g/<code>` → sign in (the invite survives sign-in, as with bills) → `join_group(code)`.
3. Add costs:
   - **Receipt:** Scan or Enter items, with "Group: Tahoe" chosen → `create_bill(…, p_group_id)`. Every member becomes a participant. Claiming works as today.
   - **Simple expense:** `save_expense(group, id?, title, payer, amount, method, shares jsonb)` → the DB recomputes equal/shares cents with `private.allocate` and checks exact amounts sum to the total.
4. Balances: `group_snapshot(group)` returns **one jsonb value** (bills+rows+claims+marks, expenses+shares, payments, members+Venmo handles). One row, so `max_rows = 1000` can't cut it short (**verify**, lesson F1). Then `lib/groupBalance.ts` → `lib/simplify.ts` → `lib/groupSettleView.ts` (copy and roles, so screens do no arithmetic).
5. Settle: Venmo Pay/Request per suggestion (`venmoPayUrl` / `venmoRequestUrl`, one amount each) → `record_payment(id, group, from, to, cents, source)`. The client sends the uuid, so a retry is a no-op (the SE-ENG-3 lesson).
6. Realtime: one `group:<id>` topic, broadcast like `private.broadcast_bill_change`. Group bills also broadcast to their group topic.

### The same rule in `lib/` and the DB
| Rule | `lib/` | DB | Shared boundary cases |
|---|---|---|---|
| Equal/shares expense allocation | `allocate` (`lib/split.ts`) | new `private.allocate(amount, weights[])`, largest remainder, ties to the lower index | 1 person; 3 ways of 100 (34/33/33); 3 ways of 1¢; a weight of 0; 20 parts; 99,999,999 |
| Exact amounts add up | `Σ cents = amount`, no tolerance | same | off by +1¢ and −1¢; all on one person; a $0 share |
| Payment amount | C2: 1..99,999,999 | check constraint + function | 0, 1, 99,999,999, 100,000,000 |
| Removal (L3-G) | mirrored only to decide which buttons show | `private.group_removable` | each history type alone |
| **Balances and suggestions** | **lib only** | **none, deliberately** | Listed in the spec as a one-sided rule, so nobody "finds" a gap (the K6 lesson) |

### Hard problems and spikes (do first)
1. **Balance property tests (spike, S):** 1,000 seeded random groups (both extras modes, claimable discounts, negative shares, $0 people, incomplete bills). Checks: Σ net = 0; simplification keeps every net; ≤ n−1 payments; results don't depend on input order. Degenerate cases from the lessons: one member, everyone $0, all-$0 bills, a three-way tie.
2. **Snapshot size (spike, S):** a seed group with 40 bills × 30 rows × 6 claimers. Measure the `group_snapshot` payload and latency, and confirm a jsonb return isn't capped by `max_rows`.
3. **Membership sync and locks (M):** adding a member to N bills, a bill moving in, and claims all happening at once. Extend the one lock order (group → bill → participant) from `20261124094656_people_review_fixes.sql`. Two deadlocks have already been found in this repo (lessons).
4. **Moving a bill in with marks → payments (S):** atomic, logged, and the bill's marks are cleared.
5. **Stale screens:** a suggested payment computed from old data. Recording the amount opened is fine, because any gap shows up as a new suggestion. No stale token needed.

### Security
- Every new table: RLS on; `revoke all … from anon, authenticated, public`; `grant select` to `authenticated` only where needed; **no** client insert/update/delete. All writes go through security-definer functions with `search_path = ''`, check `auth.uid()` first, and run execute for `authenticated` only.
- Non-members get `42501 not_allowed` (acceptance criteria expect `42501`). The join code is 80 random bits, DB-generated and rotatable.
- No new secrets. Contacts stay on the device. Nothing is sent to third parties apart from Venmo links the user opens.

### Third-party costs
None new. Supabase's current plan covers it (**assumption**: groups add rows, not compute).

## Worked example: 4 people, 2 receipts and 2 simple expenses

People in user-id order: **Ana < Ben < Cy < Dee**. All amounts are cents; $ amounts are for reading.

**1. Dinner at Lupa:** receipt, proportional, payer **Ana**. Dee wasn't there (she's a participant via the group and claims nothing).
- Items: Ana burger 2000, Ben pasta 1800, Cy salad 1200, nachos 1000 shared by Ana/Ben/Cy → `allocate(1000,[1,1,1])` = 334/333/333.
- Items subtotals: Ana 2334, Ben 2133, Cy 1533 (Σ 6000). Tax 533 + tip 1200 = extras 1733. Total **7733**.
- `allocate(1733, [2334, 2133, 1533, 0])` → raw 674.137 / 616.082 / 442.782 → floors 674/616/442 (Σ 1732). The 1 leftover cent goes to the largest remainder (Cy: 4689 > Ana 822 > Ben 489) → 674/616/443.
- **Shares: Ana 3008, Ben 2749, Cy 1976, Dee 0.** Σ = 7733 ✓

**2. Brunch:** receipt, split evenly, payer **Ben**, all 4 claimed.
- Items: Ana 900, Ben 1100, Cy 700, Dee 1300 (Σ 4000). Extras 1001. `allocate(1001,[1,1,1,1])` = 251/250/250/250 (the tie goes to the lower index, Ana).
- **Shares: Ana 1151, Ben 1350, Cy 950, Dee 1550.** Σ = 5001 ✓

**3. Airbnb:** simple expense, **Ana** paid 24000, Equally among 4 → **6000 each**. Σ = 24000 ✓

**4. Rental car:** simple expense, **Dee** paid 30000, split by shares (days) Ana 2, Ben 2, Cy 2, Dee 1 (v1 Shares, used here to show rounding).
- Raw 8571.43 ×3, 4285.71 → floors 8571/8571/8571/4285 (Σ 29998). Remainders 3/3/3/5, so Dee gets +1, then the tie among Ana/Ben/Cy goes to Ana.
- **Shares: Ana 8572, Ben 8571, Cy 8571, Dee 4286.** Σ = 30000 ✓

**Balances**
| | Paid | Share (1+2+3+4) | Net |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ana | 7733 + 24000 = **31733** | 3008+1151+6000+8572 = **18731** | **+13002** |
| Ben | **5001** | 2749+1350+6000+8571 = **18670** | **−13669** |
| Cy | **0** | 1976+950+6000+8571 = **17497** | **−17497** |
| Dee | **30000** | 0+1550+6000+4286 = **11836** | **+18164** |
| Σ | 66734 | 66734 | **0** ✓ |

**Suggested payments (greedy):**
1. Cy (−17497) → Dee (+18164): **17497** ($174.97). Dee has 667 left.
2. Ben (−13669) → Ana (+13002): **13002** ($130.02). Ben has −667 left.
3. Ben → Dee: **667** ($6.67).

That's 3 payments (n−1), moving 31166 in total. Afterwards every net is 0 ✓

**"Who paid what", pairwise without simplifying:** B→A 7598, C→A 7976, A→D 2572, C→B 950, B→D 7021, C→D 8571. That's **6 payments** and the same nets.

**Partial payment:** Cy records 10000 to Dee. Nets become Cy −7497, Dee +8164. The suggestions become Cy→Dee 7497, Ben→Ana 13002, Ben→Dee 667.

**Edit after everyone paid:** all 3 payments are recorded (every net is 0). Then Ana edits Dinner's tip from 1200 to 1500.
- Extras go to 2033. `allocate(2033,[2334,2133,1533,0])` → floors 790/722/519 (Σ 2031). The 2 leftover cents go to Ana (rem 5022) and Ben (4389) → 791/723/519.
- New dinner shares: 3125/2856/2052 (Σ 8033 ✓).
- Net changes: Ana +300−117 = **+183**, Ben **−107**, Cy **−76** (Σ 0 ✓).
- New suggestions: **Ben → Ana $1.07, Cy → Ana $0.76**. That's the "pay the difference" with no new mechanism.

### Recommended MVP
Trip groups that hold scanned/typed receipts (claimed by item) and quick simple expenses, with exact integer-cent balances, a short list of suggested payments, Venmo Pay/Request for each, and self-reported payments, including partial and cash.
- Create a group; invite by link, QR, People you've split with, or contacts; a Groups section on Home
- Add a receipt bill to a group (when creating it, or move one in later); every member is a participant; settling happens at group level
- Simple expenses: title, amount, paid by, split Equally (choose who) or by Exact amounts
- Balances computed in `lib/` (rule G for incomplete bills); every net adds up to $0 exactly
- Suggested payments (greedy, ≤ n−1, deterministic) plus a "Who paid what" table
- Venmo Pay / Request per suggestion; "I paid" records the amount opened; Record a payment for partial or cash; either side can undo
- Edits after paying just move balances, with a "Balances changed" notice and Activity
- Group Activity feed; USD only

### Key decisions made (recommendations)
| # | Decision | Recommendation | Rationale |
|---|---|---|---|
| D1 | Where the math lives | `lib/` only; the DB stores facts and checks rules it can verify without split math | No second `computeShares`. Same stance as ADR 0021 |
| D2 | Bill ↔ group | 0 or 1 group; add at creation or move in later (host); remove allowed | Unambiguous, and recovers from mistakes |
| D3 | Members vs participants | Every member is a participant of every group bill; a non-attendee claims nothing and owes $0 | Keeps "only participants read a bill" |
| D4 | Per-bill settling in a group | Off; marks turn into payments when a bill moves in **(you, Q1)** | One ledger, no double-counting |
| D5 | Incomplete bills (rule G) | Proportional counts (unclaimed sits with the payer); even waits; all-$0 excluded **(you, Q3)** | Reuses rule E; Σ = 0 |
| D6 | Simple expense model | Separate `expenses` + `expense_shares`; Equally + Exact in the MVP, Shares in v1 | Fixed per-person cents, no claiming needed |
| D7 | Who edits | Any member adds; the creator or payer edits/deletes an expense; receipt items stay host-only | Predictable, and logged |
| D8 | Simplify | Greedy with ties broken by user id, 1¢ ignored; "Who paid what" next to it **(you, Q4)** | Same numbers on every phone |
| D9 | Payments | Either side records or undoes; the client uuid makes a retry a no-op; partial and over-payment allowed | Cash on trips. The SE-ENG-3 lesson |
| D10 | Edits after paying | The ledger absorbs them; no `settle_difference` in groups | Simpler than today |
| D11 | Leaving | Only with no history; otherwise Hide (owner-only) | Leaving never changes money (L3) |
| D12 | Currency | USD only; currency stays a parameter in code | v2 |
| D13 | Privacy | Members see everything in the group, including Venmo handles; group name in the Venmo note behind a kill switch **(you, Q5)** | Same as a bill today |

### Risks to watch
- Friends without SplitIt blocking groups (A2). This is the most likely reason the feature fails.
- "Why do I owe Cy?" eroding trust in simplification.
- Lock-order deadlocks from membership sync (two deadlocks already in this repo).
- Snapshot size and `max_rows` silently cutting results short.
- Venmo changing its link format (ADR 0007), or rolling out item claiming in Groups.
- Rule G and the one-sided balance rule being "fixed" later by someone who doesn't know they're deliberate. Record both in the ADR.

## User decisions (2026-11-26)
1. **Group-level settling only:** yes. Bills in a group stop settling one at a time; existing "I paid" marks become group payments when a bill moves in (needs a new ADR amending 0015 and 0021).
2. **Everyone signs in:** yes for now (no account-less members in the MVP; ADR 0004 unchanged).
3. **Unfinished receipts:** unclaimed items do **not** count against the payer. A receipt adds nothing to the group balance until every item is claimed; the group shows it as e.g. "Dinner: 2 items left to claim". (Replaces the plan's proportional "settle early" rule for group bills.)
4. **Simplified payments by default**, with "Who paid what" one tap away: yes.
5. **Privacy:** yes — joining a group counts for People you've split with; members see each other's Venmo usernames; the group name goes in Venmo notes behind an off switch.

Main-session picks for the smaller choices (logged for review, per the user's build-without-blocking preference): split by Shares stays out of the MVP (Equal + Exact only); an expense's creator or payer can edit/delete it; push reminders are out (they need a server or scheduled job — a backend decision left to the user).
