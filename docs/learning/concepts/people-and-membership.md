# People and membership (pointer)

The full write-up of "people you've split with" lives in **[ADR 0016](../decisions/0016-people-come-from-shared-bills.md)**. This note is a short map of the same ideas so you can find them from the concepts list.

- **Who counts as "split with" (rule L1).** Two people are linked through a bill once both of them took part: hosted it, joined with the link, or claimed something. Being *added* by the host doesn't count on its own. Opening the link after being added counts as joining (R6). The rule lives once, in `private.my_links()` (`supabase/migrations/20261124091041_people.sql`).
- **Adding someone.** Only the host, and only someone already linked to them. A missing user and an unlinked user get the same `not_linked` answer, so the call can't be used to find out who has an account: see [row-level security](row-level-security.md), "`not_linked`: a privacy story".
- **Removing and leaving (rule L3).** Allowed only for someone with no claims, no "I paid" mark, who isn't the payer or the host, so nobody's share ever changes. The same rule is in `lib/membership.ts` and `private.is_removable` (`supabase/migrations/20261124091042_membership.sql`), tested on the same cases M1–M8 on both sides. *(Note 2026-11-27, Who paid, [ADR 0023](../decisions/0023-several-payers.md): "the payer" now means anyone in the payer set, co-payers included, and "no mark" now means every pair of payments involving you nets to $0, so someone fully paid back may leave. The rule was re-created in `supabase/migrations/20261127140001_bill_payments_functions.sql` and widened by `20261127140002_who_paid.sql`; both sides share table L1–L7. A co-payer sees the reason before tapping: "You're one of the people who paid. Ask Hana to change who paid before you leave.")*
- **Lock order.** Every function that touches a bill locks the bill row first; see [database functions](database-functions.md), "One lock order for every bill".
- **Contacts.** Picked on the phone, never uploaded; native code stays out of web: see [platform-specific files](platform-specific-files.md).

## Groups (added 2026-11-27, [ADR 0022](../decisions/0022-groups.md))
- **Joining a group counts for L1.** Two members of the same group are linked even if they share no bill (user decision U-G5). `private.my_links()` now includes co-members (`supabase/migrations/20261127060131_group_bills.sql`). In "People you've split with" such a person has `shared_bills = 0` and the caption "In a group together" (`personCaption` in `lib/people.ts`).
- **Joining is by link or QR only** (`join_group`). Nobody is put in a group without opening its link: there's no "add to group" from People (GR-16) and no "remove member" (GR-17). A group has at most 50 members.
- **Every member is on every group bill.** Joining a group adds you to all its bills, and moving a bill into a group adds every member. These rows have `added = false`, because opening the group link is your consent (GR-11). On a group bill, `join_bill`, `add_participant`, `remove_participant` and `leave_bill` are refused with `in_group` for members and non-members alike, so the bill's people always equal the group's members.
- **Leaving a group (rule C-G10)** is allowed only with no history in it: you don't host or pay a group bill, have no claim on one, aren't the payer, creator or a share of any expense (deleted ones included), and aren't part of any payment (undone ones included). A $0 equal share doesn't count (GR-N3). The last member can't leave. Like L3, leaving never changes anyone's money. Otherwise you can **hide** the group for yourself; nobody else sees that.
- **Deleting a group removes its links (2026-11-28, [ADR 0027](../decisions/0027-delete-group.md)).** Any member can delete a settled group, and it's a hard delete: the `group_members` rows and the group's receipts (with their `bill_participants` rows) are gone. `private.my_links()` builds links only from those rows, so two people linked **only** through that group drop out of each other's "People you've split with". People who also share a bill outside the group stay linked. The spec first said links would stay (DG-9), but that assumed a soft delete that kept the rows; the hard delete (DG-17) changed it. Deleting also stops the group's membership from blocking account deletion.
- **A side effect to know (GR-N5).** Because co-members are linked, a host can add a co-member to any bill *outside* the group without that person opening a link.

## Check yourself
1. Hana adds Sam to her bill, and Ali is also on it. Can Ali now add Sam to Ali's own bill? Why not?
2. Why does `add_participant` give the same error for a made-up user id as for a real person you've never split with?
3. Ana and Dee are in the Tahoe trip group but have never been on the same bill. Does Dee appear in Ana's "People you've split with"? With what caption?
4. Ben claimed one item on a group receipt. Can he leave the group? What can he do instead?
5. Ana and Dee were linked only through the Tahoe trip group. Ben deletes it. Is Dee still in Ana's "People you've split with"? What if they also shared a dinner bill outside the group?
